The Legacy of Haridwar

Founded on the banks of the holy river, Patanjal Yog Dham was established to preserve the purity of Vedic traditions while making them accessible to the contemporary seeker. For decades, our ashram has served as a bridge between the spiritual heights of the Himalayas and the everyday lives of practitioners worldwide.
